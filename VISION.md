# Product Vision — Mobile Deck-Building Card Game

## Vision

Our vision is to create a fun and accessible mobile deck-building game where players can build and customize their own decks, experiment with different card combinations, and develop their own strategies. The game will be easy for new players to understand while still providing enough variety and strategy to keep players engaged.


## Vision Statement

For mobile players who enjoy strategy and card games
who want a game that is easy to pick up but still rewards creative thinking,
our game is a mobile deck-building card game
that lets players build and customize their own decks
Unlike many card games that overwhelm new players with complex rules
our productis easy to learn while still offering enough variety.
