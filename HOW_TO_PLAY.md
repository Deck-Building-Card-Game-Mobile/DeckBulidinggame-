# How to Play — Dungeon Deck

Fight your way through a dungeon with a deck of cards. Win battles, collect new
cards, make your deck stronger, and defeat the boss at the end. If you run out
of HP, the run is over and you start again with a fresh deck.

> **Note for playtesters:** combat and cards are finished. Sections marked
> *(coming soon)* are still being designed and may change.

---

## The goal

- Clear the dungeon's encounters and defeat the **final boss**.
- Your **HP carries over** between fights, so every hit you take matters.
- Each run starts from the same simple deck. Every run, your deck grows in a
  different direction depending on the cards you pick.

## Your starting deck

You start every run with **70 HP** and these 10 cards:

| Card | Copies | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Strike | 5 | Deal 6 damage. |
| Defend | 4 | Gain 5 block. |
| Lantern Bash | 1 | Deal 5 damage. Apply 1 Vulnerable. |

These cards are basic on purpose. Almost every card you find will be better.

---

## A turn, step by step

1. **Start of your turn**
   - Any block you had left over goes away.
   - If you're poisoned, you lose HP.
   - Your energy refills to **3**.
   - You draw **5 cards**.
2. **Play cards.** Each card costs the energy shown in its corner. Play as many
   as you can afford, in any order. Cards that hit one enemy ask you to choose
   which enemy.
3. **End your turn.** Unused energy is lost, and every card left in your hand
   goes to the discard pile.
4. **Enemies act.** Each enemy does exactly what its **intent** showed.
5. Back to step 1.

When your draw pile is empty, your discard pile is shuffled to make a new one,
so you'll see your cards again and again.

## Reading enemies (intent)

Above each enemy is an icon showing what it will do **on its next turn**:

| Intent | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⚔️ Attack (with a number) | It will hit you for that much damage. Gain block to stop it. |
| 🛡️ Block | It will protect itself. Hit it now, before the block goes up. |
| ⬆️ Buff | It will make itself stronger. Try to finish it quickly. |
| ⬇️ Debuff | It will weaken you (for example, make you Weak). |

Reading intents is the core skill. If an enemy is about to attack, defend. If
it isn't, go all out on damage.

## Winning and losing a fight

- **Win:** every enemy reaches 0 HP.
- **Lose:** you reach 0 HP. The run ends.

## Understanding a card

- **Cost** (top corner): how much energy it takes to play.
- **Type**:
  - **Attack**: deals damage.
  - **Skill**: block, card draw, healing, debuffs, and other tricks.
  - **Power**: stays active for the rest of the fight once played.
- **Rarity**: Common, Uncommon, Rare or **Legendary**. Legendaries are the most
  powerful and the hardest to find, and you can only have **one copy** of each.
- **Upgraded** cards have a **+** after their name (for example, *Slash+*) and are stronger.

Tap a keyword on a card to see what it means.

## Keywords

| Keyword | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Block** | Stops incoming damage. Block disappears at the start of your next turn, so it only protects you for one enemy turn. |
| **Heal** | Restores HP, up to your maximum. |
| **Poison** | At the start of the poisoned creature's turn, it loses HP equal to its Poison, then Poison goes down by 1. Block doesn't stop it. |
| **Weak** | Deals 25% less attack damage. Lasts the listed number of turns. |
| **Vulnerable** | Takes 50% more attack damage. Lasts the listed number of turns. |
| **Strength** | Every attack hit deals +1 damage per Strength. Lasts the whole fight. |
| **Exhaust** | After you play it, the card is removed **for the rest of this fight**. It's back in your deck next fight. |
| **Power** | Stays in play for the rest of the fight instead of going to the discard pile. |
| **ALL enemies** | Hits every enemy in the fight. |
| **Discard** | Choose a card from your hand to put in the discard pile. |
| **Retain** | This card isn't discarded at the end of your turn; it stays in your hand for next turn. |
| **Unique** | You can only have one copy of this card in your deck. Every Legendary is Unique. |
| **Invulnerable** | You take no damage until your next turn. |
| **Intent** | What an enemy plans to do next turn. Some cards (like Parry) check it. |

Your hand holds at most **10 cards**. If you draw with a full hand, the extra
cards go straight to the discard pile.

## After a fight

- **Card reward:** choose **1 of 3** new cards to add to your deck, or **skip**.
  Skipping is a real choice: a smaller deck draws your best cards more often.
- **Legendary cards** show up rarely in normal rewards. After beating an
  **elite** or the **boss**, one of your three choices is always a Legendary.
- **Upgrade cards** to make them stronger *(where upgrades happen is coming soon)*.
- **Remove cards** (usually Strikes and Defends) to make your deck more
  consistent *(coming soon)*.

## Deck styles to try

Every card belongs to one of four styles (or is Neutral and fits anywhere).
Picking cards from one or two styles makes your deck work together.

| Style | Plan | Cards to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Brawler** | Build Strength, then hit many times. | Battle Cry, Twin Strike, Quick Jab, Berserker Rage, Executioner's Axe |
| **Guardian** | Stack block and turn it into damage. | Iron Skin, Bulwark, Parry, Shield Bash, Thorn Armor |
| **Venom** | Spread Poison and let it do the work. | Toxic Vial, Venom Cloud, Envenom, Catalyst, Black Plague |
| **Tactician** | Draw extra cards, gain energy, weaken enemies. | Scout Ahead, Expose, Smoke Bomb, Adrenaline, Quick Thinking |

## Beginner tips

1. **Check intents every turn.** Only block when an enemy is attacking.
2. **Apply Vulnerable before you attack.** Lantern Bash, then Strike, deals 9 instead of 6.
3. **Don't take every card.** If a reward doesn't fit your deck, skip it.
4. **Play Powers early.** They work for the rest of the fight, so the sooner the better.
5. **Poison stacks.** Venom Cloud followed by Catalyst can wipe out a tough enemy.
6. **Watch your HP between fights.** Taking a little damage to win faster is fine, but HP doesn't refill on its own.
